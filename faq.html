Frequently Asked Question

What time should I arrive?

To ensure everyone is comfortably seated before the ceremony begins, please arrive at Our Lady of the Assumption Parish Church by 12:30 PM. Our program will start promptly at 1:00 PM. For the reception at La Madre Boutique Resort, cocktail hour/doors will open at 4:00 PM.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please let us know if you can make it by submitting your response through our RSVP tab by October 27, 2026. This helps us finalize our seating and catering counts with our venues on time!

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to strict venue capacity limits, we can only accommodate the specific guests listed on your invitation. If your invitation is addressed to you "and Guest," you are absolutely welcome to bring a plus one! Otherwise, we look forward to celebrating with you individually.

Are children allowed?

To allow all of our guests—including parents—a night of relaxation and celebration, we have chosen for our wedding day to be an adults-only occasion. We love your little ones, but we hope you can use this as an opportunity to enjoy a fun night off!

A note on gifts:

Your presence on our special day is the greatest gift we could ask for! However, if you would like to honor us with a gift, a monetary contribution toward our future home and new beginnings together would be deeply appreciated.

"We know some of these arrangements require extra planning from our loved ones, especially parents. Please know that every decision was made to ensure a seamless and beautiful celebration for everyone. We deeply appreciate your love, respect, and understanding as we prepare for our wedding day!"
